CollabLLM: From Passive Responders to Active Collaborators

Shirley Wu1, Michel Galley2, Baolin Peng2, Hao Cheng2, Gavin Li1, Yao Dou3, Weixin Cai1, James Zou1, Jure Leskovec1, Jianfeng Gao2

1Stanford University, 2Microsoft, 3Georgia Tech

ICML 2025 Outstanding Paper

Presented by Iuliia Korotkova, Yash Bhardwaj and Elgun Hasanov

Hi everyone. We are presenting CollabLLM, by Shirley Wu and colleagues from Stanford, Microsoft and Georgia Tech. It received an Outstanding Paper award at ICML 2025. The title sums up the paper: today's LLMs are passive responders. They answer whatever we type, even when the request is vague. This paper trains them to act as collaborators that ask, suggest and steer the conversation toward what the user actually wants. I'll start with why current models behave this way. Yash will explain the method, Elgun the results, and Yash will then present our own analysis of the paper's limitations.
