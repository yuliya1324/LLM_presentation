To finish, here is how we built these slides. We used one repo with Claude Code, and every step reads the files the previous step wrote. Step one, fetch: the /ingest command downloaded the paper, its LaTeX source, the code and the project page. It wrote shared notes in sources/notes: the paper with exact numbers and page references, the repo, the website's style and a glossary. It also exported the original figures to assets/figures. Step two, plan: from those notes we wrote outline.md, with one line per slide giving the single message that slide makes and who owns it. Step three, draft: each of us ran /draft on our own file in sections/. CLAUDE.md sets the rules: only the layouts in template/, at most 40 words per slide, numbers that match the paper with a source, and the orange colour reserved for CollabLLM. Step four, iterate: build.py merges the sections into one reveal.js deck and screenshots every slide. We checked the screenshots and fixed each slide with /revise, one click at a time, then ran /review across the whole deck as a final check. The rules worked like a reward we could check, so the three of us produced one consistent deck. Thank you, we are happy to take questions.
