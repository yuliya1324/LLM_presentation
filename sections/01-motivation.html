Why does this happen, and how do the authors fix it?

The reason is how models are trained. Here is standard RLHF. We take a context x and a response y, rate the response at the next turn with thumbs up or down, and train the policy on these single-turn rewards.

Click: in a conversation, the user has an implicit goal g, and each turn is a user input u and a model response m. Under RLHF every model response is scored on its own, right after it is written. Under that signal, a clarifying question looks worse than a full answer, because it doesn't give the user anything yet. Click: the paper's key motivation. Summing good single-turn rewards over the K turns does not guarantee a high conversation-level reward R star given the goal g. An article written section by section can look helpful at every turn and still lack coherence at the end.
