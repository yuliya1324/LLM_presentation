Two forward turns balance gains and sampling cost

$w = 2$: BLEU 0.373 · 1.85k tokens · ITR 0.70

Fig. 4, p. 6; Table 7, p. 15; full ablation: Fig. 9, p. 16

This is the paper's reward ablation, separate from our own experiments. Figure 4 tests document editing. With two forward turns, BLEU reaches 0.373, token use falls to 1.85 thousand, and interactivity reaches 0.70. Three turns leave BLEU unchanged, save only 0.01 thousand tokens and slightly reduce ITR. Table 7 also shows the added sampling cost: policy time per forward sample rises from 15.84 to 21.72 seconds between two and three turns. This supports the authors' choice of two turns for their main setup, rather than a universal optimum. Figure 9 reports different immediate-reward values, so the numbers on this slide come specifically from Figure 4.
