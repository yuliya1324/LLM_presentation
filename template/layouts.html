Opening line.

Make your LLMs active collaborators

A fine-tuning framework that trains LLMs for effective, efficient multiturn collaboration with users.

What to say.

Single-turn rewards teach models to answer, not to collaborate

?

What is missing

Faced with ambiguous requests, LLMs respond passively instead of helping users work out what they need.

1

Why it happens

RLHF rewards each response in isolation, so asking a clarifying question looks worse than answering.

∞

The fix

Reward a response by its long-term effect on the whole conversation.

What to say.

A response is worth the conversation it leads to

$$\mathrm{MR}(m_j \mid t^h_j, g) = \mathbb{E}_{t^f_j \sim P(t^f_j \mid t_{1:j})}\, R^*(t_{1:j} \cup t^f_j \mid g)$$
  • $t^f_j$: future turns, sampled with a user simulator
  • $R^* \approx R_{\text{ext}} + R_{\text{int}}$: task success plus efficiency and engagement
